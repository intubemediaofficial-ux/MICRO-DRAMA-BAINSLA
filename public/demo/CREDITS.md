# Demo artwork

Fictional placeholder artwork for the seeded demo catalogue. Every series, title,
character and cast name here is invented — no real person, brand or production is
depicted, and each poster carries a `DEMO ARTWORK` marker.

Posters, banners and episode thumbnails are composited by
`scripts/demo-artwork/generate.py` (Pillow) from CC0 / public-domain photographs
that are heavily duotoned, blurred and overlaid, so no recognisable individual
remains. Cast photos are generated monograms, not photographs of people.
